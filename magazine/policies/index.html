Privacy and ordering information

Privacy notice for magazine inquiries

When you send an inquiry from the magazine page, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, the keepsake and occasion you are interested in, an optional quantity and date, your project notes, and whether you asked to receive marketing emails.

We use these details to reply to your inquiry and to plan your project with you. We do not sell your information. If you opt in to marketing emails, we may also send occasional updates, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Please do not include payment details, health information, or children's personal details in an inquiry. We do not accept photo uploads through this form.

To ask about, correct, or delete the information you sent, email hello@lyfemoment.store.

Ordering, shipping, and returns

Personalized magazines and keepsakes are not yet available to order online. Formats, prices, production times, shipping, and return or revision terms will be published here and on each product page before ordering opens.

Questions in the meantime? Email breadoflyfepublishing@gmail.com.
