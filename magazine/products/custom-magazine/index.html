Custom Magazine

A personalized magazine concept built around someone's photos, stories, and milestones.

This is the heart of the collection: a magazine made around one person, one couple, or one group of friends. Picture a cover with their name in the masthead, pages that move through the moments you choose, and words that sound like the people who know them best. It is a keepsake meant to be read, passed around, and saved, whether it becomes a custom birthday magazine, an anniversary magazine, a friendship magazine, or a graduation keepsake.

A good fit for

A centerpiece gift for a milestone birthday, anniversary, graduation, or a celebration of someone you love.

What you can personalize

  • Cover title and masthead name
  • Cover photo
  • Photos throughout the pages
  • Personal messages and letters
  • Captions, inside jokes, and favorite quotes
