The First Year Issue

Twelve months of firsts, kept in one place.

A first-year magazine for a growing family. Month-by-month photos, milestones and the little notes parents always mean to write down, gathered into an issue the family can read now and pass down later.

Choose how you want it

  • DIY Template

    An editable template you fill in with your own photos and words. Every page is already designed.

    1. Choose your edition and check out.
    2. Open your editable template from the link you receive.
    3. Add your photos, names and words. Every page is laid out for you.
    4. Download your finished file and print at home or with a print shop of your choice.

    Ordering opens soon. Ask us a question in the meantime.

  • Made for You

    Send us your photos and stories. Our team designs your issue, prints it and ships it to you.

    1. Send an inquiry and tell us who the issue is for.
    2. We confirm the details, timing and price with you before we begin.
    3. We share how to send your photos and words.
    4. Our team designs your issue, prints it and ships it to you.

    Ordering opens soon. Ask us a question in the meantime.
