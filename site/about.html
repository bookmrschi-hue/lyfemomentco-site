About the house

We print what is true, honorable and lovely.

Lyfe Moment is the keepsake magazine house of Bread of Lyfe Publishing. We believe the people in your life deserve to be written about with the same care as anyone on a newsstand.

Every issue begins with listening. We ask good questions, write with honesty and warmth, and design each page so it feels like a real magazine, because to the person on the cover, it is.
