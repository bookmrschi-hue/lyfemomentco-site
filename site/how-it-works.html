How it works

One conversation. One issue.

You give us about two hours and a folder of photos. We do the reporting, writing, design and print, and nothing goes to print until you approve it.

  1. Consultation

    A short call with you or your assistant to agree on the edition, the angle, the deadline and who receives copies.

  2. The interview

    Sixty to ninety minutes with your editor. We prepare the questions; you just talk. Optional short interviews with family, colleagues or friends.

  3. Writing and design

    We write the feature, select and art direct your photos, and lay out the cover and spreads.

  4. Your proof

    You review a digital proof of the full issue. Edits are collected in one round, so nothing goes to print without your sign-off.

  5. Print and delivery

    Your run is printed softcover and delivered, ready to give. Additional copies can be shipped directly to your list.

What we need from you

Easy to hand off.

  • Your time for one interview, or the recipient's if it is a gift.
  • Photos you love, shared through a private link we send you.
  • Names and contact details for anyone you would like us to interview.
  • The date you need it and where copies should go.

An assistant, manager or family member can handle every step except the interview itself.
