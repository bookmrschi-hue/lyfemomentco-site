Privacy & discretion

Your story stays yours.

This is a plain-language summary of how we treat what you share with us. [Have the final privacy policy and terms reviewed before launch.]

Confidentiality

A confidentiality agreement is offered with every Year in Review and Private Commission. We will sign yours, or provide ours, before any interview. Interview recordings and notes are used only to write your issue.

Your photos and words

Photos and materials you send are stored privately and used only for your issue. We delete your photos and files 60 days after your issue is delivered, or sooner if you ask.

Printing

Your issue is printed in a private run for you and your list. It is never sold, listed publicly or reprinted for anyone else.

Marketing

We never show, share or use your issue in our marketing unless you give written permission.

Website inquiries

Details you send through our consultation form are used to reply to you and prepare your consultation. We do not sell your information.
