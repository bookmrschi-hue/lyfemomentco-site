The process

Five steps. About two hours of your time.

You give us one conversation and a folder of photos. We do the reporting, writing, design and print.

  1. Private consultation

    A short call with you or your assistant to agree on the edition, the angle, the deadline and who receives copies.

  2. The interview

    Sixty to ninety minutes with your editor. We prepare the questions; you just talk. Optional short interviews with family, colleagues or friends.

  3. Writing and design

    We write the feature, select and art direct your photos, and lay out the cover and spreads.

  4. Your proof

    You review a digital proof of the full issue. Edits and approvals are collected in one round, so nothing goes to print without your sign-off.

  5. Print and delivery

    Your private run is printed and delivered, ready to give. Additional copies can be shipped directly to your list.

Questions

Before you commission.

How is this different from a photo book app?

An app arranges photos you already have. We interview you, write an original feature story and design it as a magazine. You are paying for the reporting, the writing, the art direction and the privacy, not a layout tool.

How much of my time does it take?

Usually a short consultation, one interview of sixty to ninety minutes, and a review of your proof. An assistant can handle scheduling, photo collection and delivery details.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes, and most editions are gifts. We can interview the people around the recipient and keep the issue a surprise until it is delivered.

How long does it take?

Your timeline is confirmed at consultation. For holiday delivery, commissions close on [December date].

Is the print hardcover?

Launch editions are printed softcover on heavy magazine stock, the way a real issue is printed. Additional copies can be added at any time.

How does payment work?

After your consultation you receive a secure invoice. Your commission is confirmed once it is paid. [Deposit or pay-in-full policy to confirm.]
